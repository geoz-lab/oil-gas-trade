Oil & Gas Multi-Agent Supply-Chain Forecasting

Statistical baseline · Constitutional signal filtering · Adversarial debate · Temporal reasoning

Baseline answers: How large?

LightGBM quantile ensemble (2Y + 1Y + 6M windows) provides statistically calibrated price levels and confidence intervals — the scale anchor before any LLM reasoning.

Signal pipeline answers: Which direction & when?

Multi-source signals filtered by a Constitutional AI judge, structured as evidence, reasoned temporally, then debated by four adversarial agents to drive directional adjustments.

Step-by-Step Detail

01
Task Planner
One LLM call decomposes the natural-language query into structured intent. Extracts commodity, region, forecast variable, and horizon weeks. Prevents downstream agents from misinterpreting scope.

02
Baseline Forecast — LightGBM Quantile Ensemble
Trains five quantile models (q05/q10/q50/q90/q95) across three historical windows (2Y, 1Y, 6M). Walk-forward validation assigns each window a weight based on directional accuracy. Recursive multi-step forecast extends 8 weeks, appending the q50 prediction to the rolling feature window at each step. The baseline runs before any LLM reasoning to prevent anchoring bias — it provides the price level, not the direction.
BaselineForecast price scale anchor CI bands q05–q95 per-window weights walk-forward RMSE

03
Signal Retrieval — Multi-Source with LLM Keyword Expansion
Step 1 — Keyword expansion: LLM generates 12 commodity-specific search phrases covering supply/demand drivers, geopolitical risks, refinery operations, macro indicators, and seasonal factors. Cached 24 hours.
Step 2 — Data pull: yfinance (Brent, WTI, HO, RB, NG futures + Brent–WTI spread), EIA API v2 (US crude/distillate/gasoline/PADD3 stocks), Alpha Vantage (CPI, real GDP, unemployment), NewsAPI (6 queries = 4 base + 2 from LLM keywords), EIA weekly petroleum text, OPEC press room.
Step 3 — Recency filter: NewsAPI articles older than 6 months are discarded before caching.
Step 4 — Cache: All signals + raw articles saved to data/cache/YYYYMMDD_signal.json.

Signal Judge — Constitutional AI Quality Filter
Every signal is evaluated against five quality criteria (see section below). Signals are rated reliable / questionable / misleading. Misleading signals are removed from the bundle. Questionable signals have their confidence halved before passing to evidence structuring. Processes in batches of 20 signals per LLM call.

04
Event Detection
Rolling Z-score anomaly detection on price and inventory series. LLM scans filtered headlines for supply disruptions, sanctions, refinery outages, port congestion, weather events, and OPEC decisions. Each event carries type, severity, affected region, supply impact, and confidence score.
EventListseveritysupply_impactstatistical_anomalies

05
Evidence Structuring
LLM classifies each signal into four tiers. FACT-class items (verifiable, dated, sourced) feed the debate at full confidence weight. Causal claims are marked as argued connections with potential lag. Speculations are analyst opinions — kept but downweighted. This prevents unverified narratives from dominating the debate.
FACT CAUSAL_CLAIM SPECULATION FORECAST dominant_narrative

06
Temporal Reasoning
For each causal claim, the LLM estimates: how many weeks until the price/inventory effect materializes (impact_lag_weeks), how long it persists (persistence_weeks), and the step-by-step propagation path. Example: "OPEC cut → tanker utilization rises (W1–2) → Gulf Coast refinery import cost up (W3–4) → inventory draw (W5–6), onset W6, duration 4 weeks." These annotated onset weeks appear on the forecast chart.

07
Multi-Agent Adversarial Debate
Four LLM agents with opposing mandates run concurrently via ThreadPoolExecutor (4 workers). Each agent sees the same evidence but argues from its assigned perspective. A fifth synthesis call produces the consensus direction, agreement score (0–1), strongest bull/bear arguments, and skeptic flags. The adversarial structure prevents the LLM from defaulting to the most salient narrative in the news.

08
Risk Calibration
Converts the debate outcome into numeric uncertainty parameters. High agent agreement → high directional confidence. Skeptic objections → wider confidence intervals. Maps to: directional_confidence (0–1), interval_multiplier (applied to CI bands), direction_bias_pct (signed % added to median), uncertainty_regime (low / moderate / high / extreme).
CalibrationResultdirectional_confidenceCI multiplieruncertainty_regime
▼
09
Forecast Adjustment
Applies calibration to the statistical baseline. Adds direction_bias_pct to the median point forecast (week by week with compounding). Scales CI width by interval_multiplier. Generates three scenario series (bull / base / bear) with distinct probability weights summing to 1.0. The baseline remains the price scale anchor; signals determine direction and confidence.
AdjustedForecast bull scenario base scenario bear scenario 8-week weekly series
▼
RL
RL Trading Robot — Qwen2.5-0.5B + REINFORCE
Architecture: Frozen Qwen2.5-0.5B encoder (896-dim hidden, ~494M params) with an attention-mask-weighted mean pool. Trainable 3-layer MLP policy head (247K params): Linear(896→256) → LayerNorm → GELU → Dropout(0.15) → Linear(256→64) → GELU → Linear(64→3). Only the head trains; Qwen weights are frozen.

Training: REINFORCE on 3 years of weekly price sliding windows (step=2). Reward: +1.0 correct direction, -1.0 wrong, +0.3 hold-on-flat, -0.2 hold-on-trend. Variance reduced via exponential moving-average baseline (α=0.05). Adam optimizer, lr=3e-4, gradient norm clip 1.0. Best checkpoint saved by directional accuracy to robot/checkpoints/trading_policy.pt.

Inference: The full pipeline state (baseline stats, signal quality, evidence structure, debate summary, temporal dynamics, adjusted forecast) is serialized as structured text and fed to the Qwen encoder. Greedy argmax gives the action; softmax gives action probabilities.

Combined signal: Weighted vote — statistical baseline (25%) + debate consensus (45%, scaled by agreement score) + robot (30%, scaled by robot confidence). Score > +0.20 → UP; score < −0.20 → DOWN; otherwise HOLD.
RobotDecision REINFORCE Qwen2.5-0.5B combined signal trading_policy.pt
▼

An independent reinforcement-learning signal that learns directional trading decisions from 3 years of historical price data, then reads the full pipeline state at inference time to vote alongside the statistical baseline and multi-agent debate.

Signal Judge — Constitutional AI Principles

Inspired by RLHF / Constitutional AI, every retrieved signal is evaluated against five quality criteria before it can influence the forecast. Energy market news is frequently exaggerated or politically motivated; this filter prevents misleading signals from biasing the adversarial debate.

🔍
Accuracy
Is the claim factually verifiable from a primary source, or is it rumour / unverified speculation?
📅
Recency
Is the information actionable? Articles published more than 6 months ago are flagged "outdated".
🏛️
Credibility
Is the source independent (Reuters, EIA, official body) vs. a party with financial or political incentive?
⚖️
Proportionality
Is the claimed price impact proportional to the supporting evidence, or wildly exaggerated?
🔗
Independence
Is this a coordinated market-moving narrative, circular reference, or geopolitical propaganda?
Harm FlagWhat It Catches
exaggeratedPrice impact claim far exceeds what the underlying evidence supports
unverifiedNo verifiable primary source for the claim
outdatedInformation is more than 6 months old, not actionable for near-term forecast
single_sourceOnly one source, no independent corroboration
market_biasAppears designed to move price sentiment (pump/dump patterns)
geopolitical_propagandaPolitically motivated energy market narrative, not factual reporting
circular_referenceSources citing each other without any underlying primary data
